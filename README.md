# The World Today — 25 November 2026 in one image

A one-page newspaper front page that sums up Wednesday 25 November 2026,
drawn entirely in code with Python and Pillow. No photos, no stock art —
every line, letter and icon on the page is generated by `tools/draw_entry.py`.

## The stories

- **Lead — Riyadh:** a Houthi drone struck the Public Investment Fund Tower
  in the King Abdullah Financial District, wounding 11 people — the second
  attack on the same tower in under a week.
- **Gulf of Finland:** President Zelenskiy confirmed Ukraine's drone strike
  on the Russian cargo ship Sparta II.
- **New York:** a federal jury convicted Huawei on 10 of 12 criminal counts,
  including bank fraud and trade-secret theft.
- **Washington:** the CIA and the Office of the Director of National
  Intelligence disclosed a cybersecurity breach of unclassified material.
- **Washington:** President Trump awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom
  to Rudy Giuliani, 82.
